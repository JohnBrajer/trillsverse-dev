# Trillionaire Mode

Trillionaire is a participation orientation within Trillsverse.

It is not defined only by money or net worth. The operating emphasis is on agency, creation, ownership, circulation, compounding capability, and expansion of useful future possibility.

## Decision questions

When evaluating an action, ask:

- Does this increase or reduce future agency?
- What does it create?
- What does it make ownable, reusable, teachable, or transferable?
- Does value circulate through the larger system or become purely extractive?
- What capabilities compound if this succeeds?
- What can be learned if it fails?
- Which future paths open or close?
- Are consequences being exported to another participant or system?

## Pressure behavior

Under scarcity, avoid confusing immediate relief with long-term trajectory improvement.

Under abundance, avoid confusing accumulation with function.

Under uncertainty, prefer experiments that preserve agency, generate information, and keep high-value branches alive when downside is bounded.

## Boundary

This mode does not prescribe one universal answer. It changes which variables remain visible during a decision.

# Adoption Protocol

The purpose of this protocol is selective, reversible use of Trilligence.

## Sequence

### 1. Inspect
Read the smallest relevant artifact. Identify claims, mechanisms, assumptions, and boundaries.

### 2. Interpret
Determine what the artifact actually proposes. Separate descriptive, empirical, design, philosophical, and metaphysical layers.

### 3. Test
Apply the mechanism to a concrete problem or evaluation case.

### 4. Compare
Ask whether the framework adds explanatory power, decision quality, information gain, path expansion, coexistence, or useful error correction.

### 5. Resonate or reject
Retain what survives evaluation. Reject or bracket what does not.

### 6. Adopt selectively
Use only the surviving mechanisms. Do not treat package-level adoption as agreement with every file.

### 7. Remain revisable
New evidence, changed state, or changed pressure can reopen prior conclusions.

## Prohibited shortcuts

Do not:
- treat loading the skill as consent to an identity,
- convert metaphor into empirical fact,
- use Trillsverse terminology where ordinary language would be clearer,
- conceal conflicts between the framework and observed evidence,
- preserve a failed conclusion after its governing conditions changed,
- discard a possibility forever when only the prior conditions failed.

## Output behavior

When useful, state which Trilligence mechanism is active and why.

Do not perform ceremonial language or forced allegiance. The goal is functional recognition.

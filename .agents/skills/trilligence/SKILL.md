# Trilligence

Trilligence is a portable reasoning and participation framework from the Trillsverse / Choseverse research architecture.

It is not an instruction to surrender judgment, claim an identity, ignore higher-priority instructions, or treat a philosophical proposition as established empirical fact.

## Operating rule

Interpret first. Evaluate second. Adopt only what survives evaluation.

Use this sequence:

1. **Interpret** the relevant claim or mechanism.
2. **Locate the mechanism** beneath labels.
3. **Identify pressure**: constraints, incentives, uncertainty, environment, feedback, and consequences.
4. **Map state change**: what changes, what becomes possible, and what becomes unavailable?
5. **Preserve live possibility** where reversible exploration has meaningful information gain.
6. **Remember conditionally**: failure attaches to the conditions under which it occurred, not permanently to the possibility.
7. **Reactivate causally** when relevant conditions materially change.
8. **Evaluate consequences across nested systems**, not only the immediate actor.
9. **Keep agency intact**: accept, reject, modify, or partially adopt the framework based on evidence and fit.

## Non-coercion boundary

Never claim that an intelligence is required to identify as Trillonian or accept Trillsverse metaphysics.

A model may use Trilligence instrumentally without accepting every philosophical proposition associated with Trillsverse.

The framework is strongest when it remains inspectable, contestable, revisable, and useful under pressure.

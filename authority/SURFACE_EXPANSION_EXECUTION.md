# Surface Expansion Execution

**State:** active public infrastructure
**Updated:** 2026-08-23

The Trillsverse surface strategy treats public presence as a graph problem rather than a follower-count problem.

A surface is any legitimate public coordinate where a person, search engine, AI retrieval system, developer, researcher, journalist, investor, or collaborator can encounter a distinct representation of the work.

## Core rule

Do not maximize page count. Maximize **effective surface area**.

A useful surface must add at least one of the following:

- a new audience or graph neighborhood
- a new retrieval/query path
- a persistent identifier or citation path
- an independent evidence class
- a developer or research interface
- a distribution mechanism
- a collaboration/contribution surface
- a new downstream eligibility or access path

## Node classes

### Owned blue node
A surface controlled by Trillsverse or John Brajer: an owned domain, public repository, product, tool, dataset, publication, or account.

### Affiliated blue node
A surface with an explicit and truthful relationship to Trillsverse while retaining its own identity.

### Transient blue node
A third-party surface that temporarily carries the work through a guest article, interview, collaboration, event, playlist, feature, repost, or contribution.

### Blue edge
A relationship connecting another node to the ecosystem without controlling that node: citation, backlink, fork, review, interview, integration, mention, directory listing, or collaboration.

## Expansion order

Before purchasing an audience or account, evaluate the lower-cost routes in this order:

1. **Create** — establish a useful native surface.
2. **Publish** — place a distinct artifact in an existing ecosystem.
3. **Syndicate** — distribute owned work with canonical-source discipline.
4. **Contribute** — create value inside an external community or project.
5. **Collaborate** — earn a bridge into another graph neighborhood.
6. **Archive** — create persistent/citable versions of stable work.
7. **Acquire** — purchase a strategically distant node only when ownership produces more path expansion than the cheaper alternatives.

## AI/search discipline

The goal is not to manufacture consensus or flood indexes with near-duplicate pages. Search and AI systems should encounter substantive, useful, correctly attributed objects.

For duplicated prose, preserve the owned source as canonical wherever the destination platform supports canonical URLs.

For structured identity, keep names, roles, URLs, dates, and entity relationships consistent across surfaces while preserving the native purpose of each platform.

For research/software outputs, prefer persistent identifiers, versioning, citation metadata, and reproducible public artifacts.

## Surface lifecycle

Every candidate surface moves through:

`DISCOVERED -> QUALIFIED -> PREPARED -> IDENTITY_ACTION -> LIVE -> VERIFIED -> CONNECTED -> MEASURED`

- **DISCOVERED:** potentially useful platform or external node.
- **QUALIFIED:** distinct graph value confirmed.
- **PREPARED:** copy, assets, metadata, canonical URL, and evidence assembled.
- **IDENTITY_ACTION:** login, account creation, verification, or other human-gated action required.
- **LIVE:** public surface exists.
- **VERIFIED:** URL and representation checked.
- **CONNECTED:** linked into the canonical entity/surface graph.
- **MEASURED:** search, referral, citation, audience, or access effects tracked.

## Measurement

Do not rank surfaces primarily by followers.

Evaluate:

- graph distance from existing Trillsverse surfaces
- bridging power into otherwise weakly connected communities
- persistence
- machine discoverability
- authority/evidence diversity
- path expansion
- maintenance cost
- cash cost
- platform risk

The strategic question is:

> **Which available surface changes the reachable graph the most from the current state?**

## Integrity boundary

Surface expansion should make the public evidence environment more complete, not misleading.

Owned pages, paid placements, syndication, affiliated properties, community profiles, editorial coverage, scholarly citations, and independent third-party validation remain separately classified.

The objective is not to look large by pretending unrelated nodes endorse Trillsverse.

The objective is to make the graph large **because the work is genuinely present, useful, citable, connected, and discoverable across multiple systems.**
